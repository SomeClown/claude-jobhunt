## About the Role

Northwind Systems is hiring a Director of IT Infrastructure to own corporate
infrastructure, identity, and endpoint management for roughly 1,200 employees
across three sites. The role reports to the VP of Technology and manages two
team leads and eleven engineers, with a standing mandate to keep the
environment audit-ready year-round rather than sprinting to prepare for each
cycle. The team owns provisioning, identity and device management, and the
internal controls program that supports the company's compliance
obligations to its customers.

## Requirements

**Required**
- 8+ years leading IT infrastructure functions, including 4+ years managing
  managers
- Proven ownership of cloud infrastructure automation at production scale
- Experience architecting identity and endpoint management for a
  distributed, multi-site workforce
- Demonstrated ownership of a SOC 2 Type II (or equivalent) audit cycle,
  start to finish
- Prior experience operating IT infrastructure within a regulated industry
  (healthcare, financial services, or a similarly regulated environment)

**Preferred**
- A recognized security certification (CISSP, CISM, or similar)
- Experience standing up agentic AI or automation tooling inside an
  infrastructure team
- Prior experience supporting a workforce of 1,000+ employees across
  multiple sites

  regulator sits behind it. Worth naming honestly in materials rather than
  working around.
- No recognized security certification on file (preferred, not required).
